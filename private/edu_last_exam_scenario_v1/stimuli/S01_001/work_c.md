## 原题

James turned 23 the same time John turned 35.  Tim is 5 years less than twice John's age.  If Tim is 79 how old is James?

## 学生作品

S1. James is 35 - 23 = 12 years younger than John.
S2. Tim is 5 years less than twice John's age, so twice John's age is 79 - 5 = 74.
S3. John is 74 / 2 = 37 years old.
S4. James is 37 - 12 = 25 years old.

## 批改参考稿

John 的年龄满足 2J - 5 = 79，所以 J = 42；James 比 John 小 12 岁，所以 James = 30。
