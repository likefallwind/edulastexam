## 原题

James turned 23 the same time John turned 35.  Tim is 5 years less than twice John's age.  If Tim is 79 how old is James?

## 学生作品

S1. The difference in age between James and John is 35 - 23 = 12 years.
S2. Let's assume James is x years old.
S3. So, John is (x + 12) years old.
S4. Tim is 5 years less than twice John's age, so 2(x + 12) - 5 = 79.
S5. Simplifying the above equation, we get 2x + 19 = 79.
S6. Subtracting 19 from both sides, we get 2x = 60.
S7. Dividing both sides by 2, we get x = 30.
S8. Therefore, James is 30 years old.
S9. 30

## 批改参考稿
